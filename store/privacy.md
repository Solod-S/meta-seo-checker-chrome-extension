# Privacy Policy for META SEO Checker

**Last updated:** August 2026

**META SEO Checker** is designed from the ground up with a privacy-first approach.

### 1. Local-Only Processing
All page analysis, extraction of metadata, headings, images, links, and structured data is conducted **locally in your browser** on the active tab after you click the extension icon.

### 2. No Data Collection or Transmission
- **No External Servers:** META SEO Checker does not operate any backend servers and does not transmit page URLs, content, or metadata over the internet.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** Version 1.0 does not include any third-party tracking scripts, analytics, or behavioral telemetry.
- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details to use the extension.

### 3. Permissions Usage
- `activeTab`: Granted only upon clicking the extension action icon to inspect the currently active page.
- `scripting`: Used exclusively to inject the in-page DOM analyzer and to provide temporary on-page element visual highlighting.

### 4. Contact
For questions or issues regarding privacy and security, please open an issue in the official project repository.
